Mental Health in the Digital Age

About This Survey

Australians are spending more time online than ever before, whether scrolling social media, working remotely, or staying constantly connected through notifications. At the same time, rates of stress, anxiety, and burnout continue to rise across the country. Researchers at CQUniversity are investigating how our digital habits and modern work arrangements are shaping mental wellbeing, and we need your help to understand the full picture.

This short survey asks about your social media use, work arrangements, stress levels, and general wellbeing. It takes about 5 minutes to complete, and your responses are completely anonymous. There are no right or wrong answers — we are simply interested in your honest experience.

Your input will help guide future social innovation projects aimed at supporting healthier digital habits and better work-life balance for everyday Australians. We appreciate you taking the time to share your perspective.
