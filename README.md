# Mental Health in the Digital Age — Survey

COIT12207 Assignment 1

## Topic

This survey explores how digital habits (social media use, remote/hybrid work) relate to
stress and mental wellbeing.

## Files

- `index.html` — landing page with project introduction and a link to the survey
- `survey.html` — the survey form (12 questions + mandatory participant details)
- `css/style.css` — stylesheet for both pages
- `js/script.js` — form validation and results display logic

## How to View

Open `index.html` in a web browser, then click "Take the Survey" to go to `survey.html`.
Fill in the form and submit — validation errors (if any) will be shown inline, and on
successful submission your answers will be displayed on the same page along with a thank-you
message.

## Form Elements Used

Text field, select (dropdown), radio buttons, checkboxes, textarea, range slider, and submit
button.

## Validation

The six mandatory participant fields (full name, sex, age group, state, email, mobile phone)
are validated on submission. Email and mobile phone are checked against a correct format.
